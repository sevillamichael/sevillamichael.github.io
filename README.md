# Colección Panini

My Panini World Cup collection, from 1970 to 2026, turned into a website.

**→ [sevillamichael.github.io](https://sevillamichael.github.io)**

## What I collect

- **Empty albums**: Panini World Cup albums, kept blank.
- **Stickers (postales)**: complete sticker sets for most World Cups; the oldest ones (1970 to 1982) are still in progress.
- **Sealed packets (sobres)**: unopened, across years, countries and print runs.
- **Sets**: complete promo and special sets, like the McDonald's ones.
- **Sheets (hojas)**: uncut sheets and update sheets.
- **Three players**: Lionel Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo and Kylian Mbappé. Every World Cup sticker of theirs, variants included (colours, gold, print country), each marked as owned, on its way, or still missing.

## Every photo is real

Every image on the site is a photo of a piece in my own collection, front and back, taken by me. No stock images, nothing borrowed from other collectors, nothing generated by AI. The photos are only straightened, cropped and set on a dark background.

## How it's made

I started this site, and Claude (Anthropic's AI, in Claude Code) has carried it on with me since: the design, the code, and the scripts that turn my photos into what you see here. It's plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, hosted on GitHub Pages.
